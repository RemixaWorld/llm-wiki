Welcome to the exciting world where fashion meets generative AI! In this post, we're diving into how you can use this cutting-edge technology to create unique fashion designs, specifically through training a Stable Diffusion model. In the guide below, we'll explore the use of fashion clothes and demos. For a detailed training guide, please refer to our tutorial here.

Let's start with a practical example: imagine designing something as elegant as Tom Ford's Draped Silk Cape. We have single photo of product as seen below:

![Image](/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcdn.sanity.io%2Fimages%2Fd5stn9o1%2Fproduction%2F1f85e27483ad2e321eb1e0611871617949fe98cb-644x1760.png%3Fw%3D644%26auto%3Dformat&w=3840&q=75)

Original photo from Bergdorf Goodman

The process is surprisingly straightforward. You begin with a single image of the garment, ideally sourced online. To give the AI a well-rounded view, take multiple photos from different angles and zoom levels—aim for 25 varied shots to capture the essence of the piece.

![Image](/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcdn.sanity.io%2Fimages%2Fd5stn9o1%2Fproduction%2Fea25ebb2fbedf981476cac90379ce1283f6bf234-2010x815.png%3Fw%3D2000%26auto%3Dformat&w=3840&q=75)

Once you have your images, it's time to train the Stable Diffusion model. Don't worry if you're new to this; there's a step-by-step guide available here to help you through the process. Following these instructions, you'll create a custom model capable of generating innovative designs inspired by your original piece.

And there you have it—an AI-generated fashion piece that blends the classic appeal of Tom Ford with a fresh, creative twist. This is just the beginning of what's possible when you merge AI with fashion design.
