# IBM Granite

Granite is a family of open, enterprise-grade AI models that are performant, efficient, and trustworthy.

- 🤖 Granite SLMs - Our latest language models, faster, leaner, and built for agentic workloads.
- 👁️🗨️ Granite Vision - VLM with a special emphasis on document-related tasks.
- 🐥 Granite Docling - Document models for enterprise document workflows.
- 🗣️ Granite Speech - Models for automatic speech recognition and spoken language understanding.
- 📚 Granite Embedding - High-quality embedding models for RAG and semantic search.
- 🦺 Granite Guardian - Safety and content moderation models for responsible AI deployments.
- 📈 Granite Time Series - Models purpose-built for enterprise time series data.
- 🧩 Granite Libraries - Libraries of adapters that supercharge a wide range of capabilities.
