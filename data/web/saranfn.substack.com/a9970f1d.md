## **Why “Just Learn AI” Is the Wrong Goal**

McKinsey **estimates 60–70%** of tasks **knowledge** workers perform today are **automatable** with current AI. Right now, not in **2030**.

The average **knowledge** **worker** spends **28%** of their week on tasks AI can already handle. Some haven’t redesigned a single one.

A Harvard/BCG study on AI-assisted work found workers who integrated AI into their workflows saw a **40% reduction** in task-completion time and **18%** quality improvement. 

Workers who “used AI sometimes” saw nothing.

The missing piece is is **workflow** **design**.

## **The Concept: Workflows Beat Knowledge**

A workflow is: 

**trigger → context → action → output.**

> A **recurring** task has a **trigger** (it needs doing), **context** (your data, draft, or documents), an **action** (summarize, review, generate), and an **output** (your notes, inbox, or code review).

AI changes what’s **possible** in each step. But only if you build the loop. Most people find the action step and stop there.

Knowing how to write a **prompt** and having a **workflow** that runs that **prompt** at the right **moment** are **different** things.

## **Three Workflows You Can Build This Week**

#### Workflow 1: The Slash Command (Manual Trigger, Loaded Context)

![image](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!CLH6!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fcc039051-acff-4b74-a06d-886c6a142356_1254x1254.png) 

A slash command is a pre-written **prompt** you fire with a single typed word.

Create a file called **`brainstorm.md`** in your **`.claude/comma**`ds/` folder. `

Put in it: your audience, your voice rules, what you want generated, and the format you want back. 

Type `/brainstorm and Claude` Code reads the whole file before generating anything.

E.g for **entrepreneurs** building with **AI** , this is useful because you probably repeat the same thinking **patterns** every week: brainstorming offers, validating product ideas, writing content, improving onboarding, analyzing customer pain points, or planning experiments.
    
    
    # /brainstorm
    
    You’re helping me brainstorm practical AI business and content ideas.
    
    Before generating ideas, review the following folders for context:
    
    @notes/customer-research/
    
    @notes/content-ideas/
    
    @business/offers/
    
    @products/experiments/
    
    Use these folders to understand:
    
    - What my audience is currently struggling with
    
    - What ideas I have already explored
    
    - What offers, products, or services I am building
    
    - What language my audience uses
    
    - What examples, pain points, or patterns keep showing up
    
    My audience:
    
    Entrepreneurs, AI consultants, solo founders, and technical builders who want to use AI to create leverage in their business.
    
    They care about:
    
    - Finding profitable AI use cases
    
    - Building AI-powered products or services
    
    - Automating repetitive business workflows
    
    - Improving sales, marketing, delivery, or operations with AI
    
    - Turning technical knowledge into commercial offers
    
    Avoid:
    
    - Generic AI hype
    
    - Beginner explanations
    
    - Abstract trend commentary
    
    - Ideas with no clear business application
    
    - Repeating ideas that already exist in the referenced folders unless you can improve the angle
    
    Voice:
    
    Clear, practical, direct, and commercially aware.
    
    Assume the reader is smart but busy.
    
    For each idea, return:
    
    1. Working title
    
    2. Target reader
    
    3. Core business problem
    
    4. Why this matters now
    
    5. Practical AI workflow
    
    6. What the reader will build, test, automate, or improve
    
    7. Possible monetization angle
    
    8. Which folder or note inspired the idea
    
    Generate 5 ideas.
    
    Prioritize ideas that could become:
    
    - Newsletter articles
    
    - Consulting offers
    
    - Workshops
    
    - Productized services
    
    - Internal business automations

**What changes:** you stop re-explaining yourself every time. The **brainstorm** starts in the first response instead of the third.

**Updated Context:** The context is always updated, since you ask it to review folders for context:

#### Workflow 2: The Scheduled/Recurring Prompt (Zero-Touch, Runs on Its Own)

This one runs without you.

![](/img/missing-image.png)

![image](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!M5Mn!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fb561c41b-6a7f-4ec6-a566-2aa0b3f6630b_1254x1254.png) 

A cron job calls the Claude API with the same prompt every morning. The output goes somewhere useful (email, Notion, Obsidian, Slack). You wake up and the thing is already done.
    
    
    import anthropic
    from datetime import date
    
    client = anthropic.Anthropic()
    
    def daily_planning_digest():
        message = client.messages.create(
            model="claude-opus-4-7",
            max_tokens=1024,
            messages=[{
                "role": "user",
                "content": f"""Today is {date.today()}.
    
    Give me a 5-bullet planning summary for a data scientist working on AI tooling.
    Include: one thing worth reading, one workflow to improve, one risk to watch.
    Be specific. No filler."""
            }]
        )
        print(message.content[0].text)
    
    daily_planning_digest()
    

Add `0 7 * * * python daily_planning_digest.py` to your crontab and it runs at 7am every morning without you touching it.

The prompt doesn’t depend on you remembering to run it. It fires, the output exists, you read it.

#### Workflow 3: The Skill (Autonomous, Triggered by Situation)

A **slash** **command** runs when you type it. A **skill** runs when the situation matches.

![image](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!WCof!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe182ac68-2925-4a02-9176-5c0c091e9a38_1254x1254.png) 

A **skill** is a **file** with a description and instructions. **Claude** reads the **description** and decides whether the skill applies to whatever you’re working on. You don’t have to remember to invoke it.

Useful **pattern** for **founders** : every **discovery** **call** ends with a **follow** -**up** **email**. Same shape every time. Recap, next steps, scope confirmation. 

You need to write it from scratch each time, in slightly different ways, sometimes 2 days later when half the context has faded.

A **skill** fixes this. 

Build one called `discovery-followup` that includes your voice rules, the template you actually want (recap + 3 next steps + clear ask), scope phrasing that avoids over-promising, and tone variations for different deal sizes if that matters to you.

Skill structure:
    
    
    discovery-followup/
    ├── SKILL.md (description + instructions)
    ├── voice-rules.md
    ├── templates/
    └── examples/

The SKILL.md description tells Claude when to fire:
    
    
    ---
    name: discovery-followup
    description: Drafts a follow-up email after a discovery
      or sales call. Use whenever the user shares notes from
      a client call, mentions a discovery call, or asks to
      draft a follow-up to a prospect.
    ---

What this gets you: after the **call** , you **paste** your raw notes into Claude. 

No command typed. No template loaded. Claude sees “just had a call with X” in your message, reads the skill, and drafts the email in your voice with the right structure.

What changes here: **skills** do the **trigger** work for you. The cognitive load of picking the right approach disappears.

You can install skills in claude.ai projects (settings → skills) or in Claude Code under `.claude/skills/`. Same idea either way. Start with one skill for the single most-repeated thing on your calendar.

## **Why This Makes You a Power User of AI**

**“AI fluent”** in **2026** doesn’t mean knowing which models exist or understanding attention mechanisms. It means having a small set of workflows that run consistently and produce real output.

Most **people** will keep reading about AI. Few will **redesign** a **workflow** this week. Build **three** and you’re ahead of most of your team.

I spent a long time **thinking** I needed to know more before I could build anything useful. I was wrong.
