# RAG That Drafts: How to Sharpen AI with a Draft-and-Refine Strategy

What? Even RAG needs a draft? Today, let’s take a look at a new idea.

In recent years, large language models (LLMs) have shown impressive capabilities in generating text **but often struggle with providing accurate and up-to-date information, especially for complex or knowledge-intensive queries.**

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) was introduced to address this issue by integrating information from external databases into the generation process. **However, traditional RAG systems face challenges such as increased latency and potential inaccuracies when dealing with long or complex documents.**

In this artice, we introduce a new study called “[Speculative RAG](<https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.08223v1>)”, a novel approach designed to overcome these limitations by combining**a smaller, specialized LLM** for efficient draft generation with a larger, generalist LLM for accurate verification, thereby improving both speed and accuracy in generating responses.

## Overview

Figure 1: Illustration of different RAG approaches. Source: [Speculative RAG](<https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.08223v1>).

Figure 1 provides a clear comparison of different Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) approaches in handling knowledge-intensive queries:

* [**Standard RAG**](<https://medium.com/ai-in-plain-english/a-brief-introduction-to-retrieval-augmented-generation-rag-b7eb70982891>): This method incorporates all retrieved documents into the prompt, which increases the input length and subsequently slows down the inference process.
* [**Self-Reflective RAG**](<https://ai.gopubby.com/advanced-rag-08-self-rag-c0c5b5952e0e>): This approach requires specialized instruction-tuning of the language model (LM) to generate tags for self-reflection, helping the model to better retrieve and assess relevant documents.
* [**Corrective RAG**](<https://ai.gopubby.com/advanced-rag-10-corrective-retrieval-augmented-generation-crag-3f5a140796f9>): It employs an external retrieval evaluator to refine the quality of retrieved documents, focusing on improving contextual information without necessarily enhancing the reasoning capabilities of the LM.
* [**Speculative RAG**](<https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.08223v1>): The proposed approach introduces a more efficient system by leveraging a smaller, specialized LM to generate multiple drafts in parallel from distinct subsets of retrieved documents. A larger, generalist LM then verifies these drafts, selecting the most accurate response. This method not only reduces the number of input tokens per draft but also ensures diverse perspectives are considered, significantly improving both efficiency and accuracy.

Compared to traditional RAG systems and other advanced methods like **Self-Reflective RAG** or **Corrective RAG** , Speculative RAG offers significant advantages.

* Traditional RAG systems often suffer from increased latency and inefficiencies due to the length and complexity of retrieved documents. In contrast, Speculative RAG’s approach of generating multiple drafts in parallel and focusing on diverse document subsets allows it to maintain high performance.
* Furthermore, unlike methods that require extensive instruction-tuning of generalist LLMs, Speculative RAG leverages a pre-trained generalist LLM for verification, simplifying the implementation process and reducing the risk of overfitting or forgetting.

## Detailed Workflow

As shown in **Figure 1(d)** , the Speculative RAG framework is structured around a two-step process: drafting and verification.

In the drafting phase, a smaller, specialist LLM is used to generate multiple drafts of potential answers based on diverse subsets of retrieved documents. These drafts are then verified by a larger, generalist LLM, which selects the most accurate and reliable draft to form the final response.

The detailed workflow of Speculative RAG can be broken down into several key steps, as detailed in **Algorithm 1**.

### Step 1: Document Clustering

To start, the framework begins by clustering the retrieved documents based on their relevance and content similarity to the posed query (**Algorithm 1, Line 2**). This is done using a content embedding model, such as K-Means, which groups documents into clusters that represent different perspectives or aspects related to the query. Each cluster focuses on a unique angle of the topic, ensuring that the diversity of information is captured without redundancy.

### Step 2: Subset Sampling

Once the documents are clustered, the framework samples one document from each cluster to form a subset (**Algorithm 1, Lines 5–8**). This subset serves as the input for the next phase. By ensuring that each subset contains documents from different clusters, Speculative RAG minimizes redundancy and maximizes the diversity of perspectives considered during draft generation. This step is crucial as it allows the system to cover a broad range of information without overwhelming the model with too much data.

### Step 3: Parallel Draft Generation

The core innovation of Speculative RAG lies in its parallel draft generation process. The system employs a smaller, specialized LLM, referred to as the **RAG Drafter** , to generate multiple answer drafts concurrently from each subset of documents (**Algorithm 1, Line 12**). Each draft is accompanied by a rationale that explains the reasoning behind the proposed answer. This rationale is derived directly from the documents in the subset, ensuring that each draft is well-grounded in the retrieved evidence.

This step is illustrated in **Figure 1(d)** , where the smaller LLM processes the document subsets in parallel, generating a variety of drafts. The parallel nature of this process significantly reduces the time required to produce multiple potential answers, addressing the latency issues commonly associated with traditional RAG systems.

### Step 4: Draft Verification

After generating the drafts, the framework moves to the verification phase, where a larger, generalist LLM, known as the **RAG Verifier** , evaluates each draft for accuracy and reliability (**Algorithm 1, Line 13**). The generalist LLM does not need additional instruction-tuning; it relies on its pre-trained language modeling capabilities to assess the quality of each draft.

During verification, the system calculates a confidence score for each draft based on its self-consistency (i.e., how well the draft aligns with the query) and the strength of the provided rationale. The draft with the highest confidence score is selected as the final answer (**Algorithm 1, Line 15**).

This method not only ensures that the final response is accurate but also reduces the computational burden on the larger model, as it no longer needs to process the entire set of retrieved documents in one go. Instead, it focuses on verifying a smaller number of high-quality drafts, making the process both efficient and reliable.

### Step 5: Integration of the Final Answer

The draft with the highest confidence score is integrated into the generalist LLM’s output as the final answer to the query (**Algorithm 1, Line 15**). By separating the drafting and verification processes and assigning them to specialized models, Speculative RAG achieves a balance between speed and accuracy, making it a powerful tool for handling complex, knowledge-intensive queries.

## Specialist RAG Drafter: The Engine Behind Efficient Draft Generation

In the Speculative RAG framework, **Specialist RAG Drafter** plays a crucial role in generating high-quality answer drafts.

Unlike traditional methods that rely on a single, large model to handle both retrieval and generation tasks, Speculative RAG leverages a smaller, specialized language model (LM) designed specifically for understanding and processing retrieved documents. This approach allows the system to generate accurate drafts more efficiently.

### Instruction Tuning for Specialized Tasks

The Specialist RAG Drafter is fine-tuned using a process known as **instruction tuning**. This involves training the model on specific tasks that require it to generate both an answer and a rationale based on the supporting documents. The rationale explains the reasoning behind the answer, ensuring that the response is not only correct but also grounded in the retrieved evidence.

The instruction-tuning process augments the traditional question-answering model by adding an extra layer of explanation, making the generated responses more reliable and informative. This tuning enables the Drafter to focus exclusively on understanding and summarizing the documents, making it highly efficient in generating well-reasoned answers.

### Multi-Perspective Sampling

To maximize the diversity and relevance of the drafts, the Specialist RAG Drafter employs a **multi-perspective sampling** strategy. After the retrieved documents are clustered based on their content, the Drafter selects one document from each cluster to form a subset. This method ensures that each draft is based on a variety of perspectives, reducing redundancy and increasing the likelihood that the final answer is comprehensive and accurate.

The use of multiple perspectives in the sampling process is a key innovation. It allows the Drafter to cover different angles of the query, making the generated drafts more robust and less prone to bias or error.

### Parallel Drafting for Efficiency

One of the standout features of the Specialist RAG Drafter is its ability to generate multiple drafts in parallel. By processing several document subsets simultaneously, the system significantly reduces the time required to produce potential answers. This parallel drafting not only speeds up the overall process but also ensures that the most accurate and reliable draft is quickly identified for final verification.

## Generalist RAG Verifier: Ensuring Accuracy with Robust Verification

The **Generalist RAG Verifier** is the second key component, tasked with validating the drafts generated by the Specialist RAG Drafter. While the Drafter focuses on quickly producing multiple drafts, the Verifier’s role is to ensure that only the most accurate and reliable draft is selected as the final answer.

### Evaluation with Self-Consistency and Self-Reflection

The Generalist RAG Verifier operates by evaluating each draft for self-consistency and self-reflection. **Self-consistency** refers to how well the draft aligns with the question and its own rationale. A draft that is self-consistent will have a higher probability of being accurate because it logically follows from the information provided in the retrieved documents.

To assess this, the Verifier uses the pre-trained language modeling capabilities of a generalist LLM, without requiring any additional instruction tuning. This means the Verifier leverages its inherent understanding of language to determine how likely it is that the draft and its rationale correctly answer the query.

Additionally, the Verifier employs a **self-reflection** mechanism, which involves posing a reflective question like, “Does the rationale support the answer?” The model then evaluates the likelihood of a positive response. This process helps to further filter out drafts that might seem plausible but lack strong logical support.

### Computation Method for Scoring

The Generalist RAG Verifier computes two key scores during its evaluation process:

1. **Self-Consistency Score** : This score is based on the conditional probability of generating the draft and its rationale given the query. A higher score indicates that the draft is likely self-consistent and reliable.
2. **Self-Reflection Score** : This score evaluates the draft’s alignment with the rationale through the self-reflection prompt. If the model predicts a high probability of the rationale supporting the answer, the draft receives a higher score.

The final score for each draft is a combination of these two metrics, helping the Verifier determine the best draft to select as the final answer.

The paper rigorously evaluates Speculative RAG across four benchmarks: TriviaQA, MuSiQue, PubHealth, and ARC-Challenge.

The results demonstrate that Speculative RAG consistently outperforms both standard RAG and advanced RAG systems across all datasets. For instance, in the **PubHealth** dataset, Speculative RAG improves accuracy by up to **12.97%** while reducing latency, as shown in **Figure 3**.

## Conclusion and Insights

This article has delved into the key aspects of Speculative RAG, a novel framework designed to enhance retrieval-augmented generation by optimizing the balance between accuracy and latency. By introducing a dual-model approach that leverages the strengths of both specialist and generalist LLMs, Speculative RAG sets a new standard for efficient and reliable information retrieval in knowledge-intensive tasks.

However, the requirement of training an additional drafter model adds some complexity, which could be a barrier for smaller organizations without extensive computational resources. While the experiment demonstrates promising latency results, further testing in real-world environments is necessary to validate its performance.

Looking forward, further research could explore optimizing the clustering and sampling processes or even integrating Speculative RAG with other emerging techniques to further enhance its capabilities.
