## The advantages of ChatDOC

Below are some practical features that performs better on ChatDOC than ChatPDF, or unique to ChatDOC.

## One-click traceability

AI may lies, but ChatDOC doesn’t. ChatDOC is a reliable AI reading tool, because of its backed citations of every answers. You can click the page number or footnotes to trace the answer source back to the original parts. Not only every response, but even every segment of the answer has a reference at the end for fact-check.

![image](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:700/0*VRUhl-XZx6eG5gLz.png)

ChatPDF only provides cited pages but doesn’t support inline citations, which is less reliable than ChatDOC.

## Select and Ask

On ChatDOC, you can select texts/tables/formulas to ask more specific questions, letting AI focused on a certain content to explain in detail.

![image](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:700/0*ViKHD2DRXH-lY1G2.png)

ChatPDF doesn’t support the “Select and ask” feature.

## Multi-doc chat

Have a bunch of documents on the same topic that you need to analyze together? No problem, ChatDOC supports multiple documents chatting and querying. Upload a folder of files, ask questions to all the files at the same time and then you will get summarized answers from all the files within the folder.

![image](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:700/0*WhKnD9t0aqFGrJeY.png)

Besides, you can choose to chat with partial files within the folder. Select the files and query about them, then you will get answers from the selected files.

![image](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:700/0*zGduRuPO9n_0WABk.png)

[ChatDOC](<https://chatdoc.com/?src=blog>) now supports a broad range of file formats, not only PDF documents, containing: word files(.doc, .docx), markdown, epub, txt, scanned files, websites. And the image supporting is on the planning.

* For scanned files: ChatDOC has self-innovated OCR with high precision, which can recognize the texts and tables well even in scanned files.
Here is a short video that shows you how ChatDOC performs well on scanned files: <https://twitter.com/chatdoc_ai/status/1679453294958366720>
* For websites: paste a website url to directly chat with the content on the websites without downloads.

![image](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:700/0*7ic4_bpKGCE454wc.png)

ChatPDF only supports PDF documents to chat.

## Formula recognition

As we mentioned above, [ChatDOC](<https://chatdoc.com/?src=blog>) allows users to select texts/tables/formula to ask. Here, we should especially mention formula recognition.

![image](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:700/0*qbpsqROUU4OuJTum.png)

Now matter how complicated the formula, just let AI explain it. Select the formula, or select the formula together with texts/tables to ask questions and pull out the information hidden in the formulas.

ChatPDF can not recognize formulas, which is imperfect for researchers to analyze the papers.

## Chat with papers on arxiv.org

arxiv.org gives us lots of papers to read and now you have a chance to chat with them. When viewing a paper at arxiv.org，just add “chat” before the url and press “Enter”, then ChatDOC will be summoned in a second. Here starts your AI reading through quering without any downloads.

Here is a short video that shows you how to do it: <https://twitter.com/chatdoc_ai/status/1719540954020147474>
