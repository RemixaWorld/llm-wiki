In the world of AI-generated content, there’s one recurring problem: it often sounds like a robot. While AI is brilliant at churning out ideas quickly, making that text sound _human_ — now that’s where things get tricky. Whether you’re composing social media posts, blog articles, or any other kind of content, creating an authentic, engaging tone can be a challenge when relying on AI.

The good news is, with the right guidance, AI can produce text that feels warm, relatable, and human. After experimenting with countless prompts, I’ve developed a strategy to make AI-generated text sound more natural and less rigid.

But first, let’s establish some ground rules for crafting that human touch.

## The Essential Prompt to Add Authenticity to AI Writing

This prompt I’m about to share is designed to bring your AI-generated content to life by making it sound more conversational, fluid, and approachable. This isn’t just about changing a few words — it’s about transforming the entire structure and tone of the text. Here’s how you can get your AI writing to sound more human:

## The Custom Humanizing Prompt:

_“Please rewrite the following AI-generated text to make it more approachable and engaging by following these steps:_

1. **Use conversational flow** : Write the content as though you’re speaking directly to a friend. Avoid repetitive structures and create a natural rhythm in the writing.
2. **Add a personal touch** : Bring in relatable feelings, everyday scenarios, or light humor to make the text more engaging.
3. **Use simple, clear language** : Skip complicated phrases and stick to straightforward words that everyone can understand without effort.
4. **Ask questions and engage directly** : Use rhetorical questions or address the reader to make them feel part of the conversation.
5. **Allow for ambiguity** : Don’t make everything sound certain. Let the writing reflect the complexities and nuances of real-life thinking.
6. **Avoid the following words and phrases** :
Avoid buzzwords or overused terms that feel corporate or robotic, such as: embarked, invaluable, groundbreaking, endeavor, enlightening, enhance, crucial, valuable, intricate, foster, elevate, synergy, unleash, amplify, landscape, folks, pivotal, testament, nuanced, profound, cognizant, promote, treasure trove, among others.*”

This prompt is designed to get the AI to move away from formal, stiff language and embrace more casual, approachable writing. The goal is to make the text relatable, engaging, and easy to digest, without sounding like it’s coming from a machine.

## A More Natural AI Workflow: How to Apply This in Practice

Now that you have the humanizing prompt, here’s how to put it into action to achieve the most natural and engaging AI-generated content:

1. **Choose the Text You Want to Enhance**
Whether it’s a LinkedIn post, a tweet, or even a blog intro, start by selecting a piece of content you’ve generated using an AI tool. This text will serve as your base draft.
2. **Apply the Custom Prompt**
Feed the chosen text and the custom prompt into the AI model of your choice. By providing clear guidelines, the AI will rewrite the content in a more conversational and human tone.
3. **Fine-Tune the Output**
After the AI gives you the revised text, review it carefully. Adjust any phrases that still feel awkward or robotic. You know your voice best, so make sure it shines through.
4. **Test It on Different Platforms**
This method works across various platforms. Whether you’re drafting a professional LinkedIn post or a snappy Twitter update, you’ll notice the text feels more organic, engaging, and perfectly suited to the platform’s vibe.

## Real-World Example: Transforming AI Text for Social Media

Let’s say you’re drafting a LinkedIn post about the role of AI in everyday work, and the initial AI-generated output looks like this:

* **Original AI Output** : “AI is revolutionizing how we approach tasks and workflows. It offers new efficiencies and opportunities for businesses to gain a competitive edge in the market.”

While functional, the text feels stiff and corporate. Let’s apply our custom prompt:

* **Rewritten Version** : “You know, AI is really changing the game when it comes to how we handle our daily tasks and work routines. It’s making things faster and smoother, giving businesses a chance to stay ahead and stand out from the crowd.”

The updated version sounds more conversational, relatable, and captures attention by addressing the reader directly.

## Adapting This Approach for Different Platforms

This strategy works across all social platforms, but here’s how it can be tailored for each:

* **For Twitter** :
“Feeling burned out from spending endless hours on the same old tasks? AI’s got your back. Let it handle the repetitive stuff so you can focus on the fun, creative things. More time for the good stuff, less time stuck in the grind. #AI #Productivity”
* **For Instagram** :
“Ever wonder how AI can simplify your life? Whether it’s automating small tasks or organizing your workflow, AI tools are designed to make things easier. Give it a try! #AIInAction #SmartTech”
* **For Blogs** :
“AI isn’t just about fancy tech — it’s becoming an everyday tool for simplifying work. Whether you’re automating tasks or finding new ways to streamline operations, AI is making work more efficient. The future isn’t just coming — it’s already here.”

Each version adjusts the tone and style based on the audience and platform, making the content feel authentic and suited to the specific context.

## Why This Approach Works

Making AI-generated content feel more human isn’t about changing a word here or there. It’s about adopting a strategy that focuses on natural, relatable communication. By guiding AI to avoid stiff, formal language and encouraging a more conversational style, you can create content that resonates with readers and feels much more personal.

## Wrapping Up: Transforming AI Content into Something Real

AI writing doesn’t have to feel stiff or unnatural. By using the right prompts and strategies, you can guide AI to create content that is not only clear but also compelling and human-like. Whether you’re crafting social media posts, blog articles, or emails, the key is in how you set the stage for the AI to perform.

Try using this approach in your next writing project and watch your AI-generated content transform into something that feels authentic and personal.
